# HSS Kitchens

Sales, purchasing, billing, and delivery follow kitchen-equipment work from intake through fulfillment.

## Language

**Opportunity**: An open sales deal requiring pricing or a proposal before it becomes an Order. Its agreed value may differ from the sum of its Line Items.

**Order**: Confirmed customer work, created directly from priced intake or when an Opportunity is won.

**Line Item**: One requested item that persists from Opportunity pricing through Order fulfillment. A removed Line Item stays in history but does not count toward active work.

**Purchase Order (PO)**: A purchase from one supplier for an Order. Acknowledgment confirms the purchase and establishes its Delivery.

**Delivery**: One delivery leg carrying whole Line Items. A Purchase Order may have several Deliveries after a split; HSS-stock Deliveries need no Purchase Order.

**Document**: A link or uploaded file attached to an Opportunity, Order, or Purchase Order. Intake attachments are optional and do not determine whether intake succeeds.

**Payment gate**: The payment requirement that must be satisfied before a Purchase Order is sent, based on the Order and customer deposit requirements.
